About WANDERCOIL

Two forms, one system. Our symbol is two beams that never touch, connected only by the space between them. That's how we run our divisions: independent, and unmistakably related.

Mission

Build creative technology and culture that's useful, honest and respectful of the people who use it.

Vision

A family of independent studios and products, built in Africa and recognised everywhere.

03 · Standard

What every division signs up to.

The parent sets three rules. Divisions decide everything else.

  1. RULE 01

    Respect people's data

    Collect only what the product needs, keep it close to the person, and no trackers or ad cookies on our sites.

  2. RULE 02

    Work in the real world

    Build for real devices, networks and costs, so things keep working without perfect internet.

  3. RULE 03

    Say plainly what it does

    Clear words, real screenshots and limits stated up front, in every product and every page.
