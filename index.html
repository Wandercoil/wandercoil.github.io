Our principles

Three rules. No exceptions.

Every company under Wandercoil signs up to the same three rules.

  1. 01

    Private by default

    Data stays on the device that needs it. No trackers or ad cookies on our sites, and nothing collected "just in case".

  2. 02

    Works without the internet

    Our tools run locally first, so they keep working at the venue, on campus or in the village.

  3. 03

    Plain and honest

    Clear words, real screenshots and limits stated up front, so people know what they're agreeing to.
